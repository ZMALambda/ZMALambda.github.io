MONOCULUS ALPHA FRONT-FACING PHOTO

The Monoculus Alpha is a large commercial aircraft developed by the Lambda Space Agency's branch Lambda Aeronautics. It serves as the commercial flagship of the Lambda Space Agency and Lambda Aeronautics. It's etymology is derived from Greek, mono meaning one, oculus meaning eye, and alpha being the first letter in the Greek alphabet. Thus, Monoculus Alpha means the first one-eyed commercial plane. The Monoculus Alpha is still in production and use today, as it's engineering was done in order to future-proof it.
