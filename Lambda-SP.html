LAMBDA-SP VIEWING TERMINAL

The Lambda-SP (SP being a shorthand for spaceplane) is a multipurpose vehicle that serves as both an aircraft that can carry anyone around the globe and a spacecraft that can bring anyone from anywhere between Low Earth Orbit and High Earth Orbit. There also exists a variant that is called the Lambda-SPL (SPL being a shorthand for spaceplane lunar) which serves to bridge the gap between the Earth and the Moon. The upper stage is the spaceplane itself, being comprised of multiple parts akin to the retired Space Shuttle. The lower stage is an incredibly powerful rocket that can bring the spaceplane at a certain altitude at which point it splits and returns to the ground with it's highly capable parachutes. The lower stage, known as the ApX Heavy and has a striking resemblance to the real life Super Heavy, was originally part of the ApX rocket, which was designed to carry humans to the Moon. However, after many failures, the Lambda Space Agency decided to decommission and abandon the project altogether. However, around the same time, the Lambda-SP was being built. After countless debates, it was decided upon by our engineering team (me, myself, and I) to give the ApX Heavy another chance. In the video, the plane can be seen landing on the water, however, it does have the capability to land on specialized landing pads or even runways.
