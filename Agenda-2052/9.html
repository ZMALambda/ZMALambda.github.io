9.) | Religious Institutions

  • Religious activity should generally be protected by the Philippine government in order to prevent secular division within society and terrorism in the name of religious extremism.
  • Religious institutions that are deemed violent and unlawful by the government with thorough investigations should immediately have all their activities terminated forthwith. This prevents cult activities the disrupt the flow of society.
  • Religious institutions, despite having exemptions in taxes, still heavily participate in government. An example of this can be found in the Iglesia Ni Cristo's (INC) bloc voting model which often gives them heavy leverage over the government, even being considered by critics to have lead assassinations towards officials they deem opposing to them. Another example is the Catholic Church's participation in certain government decisions. In the entire world, only 2 countries do not have a formal legal procedure to declare a divorce, those being the Vatican City, seat of the Papacy, and surprisingly, the Philippines. They are also, to a certain extent, a reason to the growing rates of teenage pregnancy since they have blocked the government's proposed aid in the form of condoms to couples. Although they have done slight setbacks for our nation, it can all be easily outweighed with how they assisted in ousting the tyrannical dictator Ferdinand Edralin Marcos Sr.
