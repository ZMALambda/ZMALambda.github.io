3.) | Economy

  • The Philippines must adopt an economic system that establishes itself upon a capitalist framework, while adopting robust egalitarian and welfarist laws to ensure equality is established, in a system known colloquially as a social democracy. Furthermore, due to it's high success rates, the Nordic model is preferred.
  • In order to preserve jobs, Artificial Intelligence, and consequently, Robotics, shall be barred to a certain extent in order to discourage the rise of unemployment among people.
  • The wealthy shall be taxed more than lower classes with loopholes that are constantly watched by the government in order to ensure equality. In the case that a wealthy man attempts to exit the country in order to evade these taxes, all their properties shall be frozen by the government and an arrest warrant to be directed to the Interpol shall be released.
  • Inheritance shall be taxed for the wealthy in order to discourage corruption solely for building generational wealth. An example of strong inheritance taxes are found in Japan where these taxes were formed after World War 2 by the American occupation under Douglas MacArthur in order to eliminate the massive gap between lower classes and the elites.
  • In turn, the lower classes shall receive lower taxes in order to ease tension and to tighten the gap of quality of life between lower class and the elites. Not only that, this gives the opportunity for the lower class to invest these taxes into worthwhile investments, which will propel the economy faster.
  • The Philippine government should lessen it's importation of grain and other crop products that are already present here in the Philippines. It should instead focus on nurturing it's own agricultural sector whilst also developing it's exports.
  • The Philippine government shall establish factories that can be leased to certain foreign companies. The workers may be gathered from poverty and put into government-provided housing for them. Their pay must be above minimum wage.
  • Speaking of minimum wage, minimum wage should be set high in order to prevent exploitation of the worker and tax rates should be inversely propotional with the minimum wage. With a wage set higher, tax rates would be lower for the company.
  • The currency should be pegged at a certain rate, lower than the dollar, in order to set the foundation for the establishment of factories and manufacturing facilities. It could also be semi-fixed, similar to China's Yuan, which imposes laws that limit the trade of the Yuan against other currencies instead of using foreign currency to peg it at a certain rate.
