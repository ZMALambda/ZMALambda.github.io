10.) | Crime

  • Criminals should be engaged in civic reprogramming thus grooming them for their release.
  • Criminals should be given jobs at factories that shall be established by the government with wages given for them to earn during their tenure in prison. Combined with workers received from the lower class, they can be a workforce that can be outsourced to any companies and would form competition with neighbouring countries.
  • Laws should be passed regarding prank calling the 911 hotline in the Philippines. Because of these prank calls, operators have to go through each prank call in order to reach actual people who have an emergency. As a result, people often call their local hotlines, which are usually lengthy and tough to memorize.
  • The Philippine government shall invest heavily into the 911 call network in order to make it resemble the 911 system in the United States of America and to reduce crime in the country.
  • Alternatively, calling 911 should automatically give out a page where you can select what emergency you need. There should also be an option to toggle your location in the case that verbal communication is not possible.
  • Vigilanteism should be awarded if the vigilante is proven to have acted with sufficient evidence. However, the proof should be thorough and well-established in order for criminals to not abuse it.
