7.) | Foreign Policy

  • The Philippines should generally be friendly to all countries. However, it should be more wary of countries that attempt to prey on it.
  • The Philippines, if given the role of leader of the Association of Southeast Asian Nation (ASEAN), should push for unity between Southeast Asian nations.
  • The Philippines, if given the role of leader of the Association of Southeast Asian Nation (ASEAN), should transform the alliance from a simple economic alliance to a more militarist one, following the model of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization.
  • The Philippines, if given the role of leader of the Association of Southeast Asian Nation (ASEAN), should opt for it to include countries like Papua New Guinea and to transform it from an alliance of Southeast Asian nations to an alliance of Asian nations.
  • The Philippines should help establish an alliance between itself and countries it has established as a solid ally akin to the Southeast Asian Treaty Organization (SEATO) during the Marcos era.
