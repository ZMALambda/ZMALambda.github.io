11.) | Healthcare

  • Healthcare, as an amenity of humanity, should be nationalized and shall not belong to any private companies for profit. In a similar fashion, health insurance and life insurance should be a guarantee by the government.
  • Free universal healthcare should be available to everyone through the use of taxes.
  • The Philippine government shall establish hospitals in rural areas where there would otherwise be limited access to healthcare. In the case that a person's health fails in the contemporary era, there would be certain death as hospitals are often concentrated in cities. Therefore, in order to service everyone and not only people in urbanized zones, rural hospitals should be built.
  • Abortion should be available to women who face fatal concerns if not treated accordingly. Abortions can also be administered if the patient is underage or the family petitions to with sufficient arguments.
  • Sexual transition surgery should be an amenity that shall be accessible to transgender people if they consent to.
  • Puberty blocking supplications should be administered with the advise of the doctor and the consent of both the parents and child patient.
  • Law shall be passed that shall declare blocking an ambulance without proper reasons a crime. Alternatively, ambulances should be permitted at all times to break traffic laws or take the alternative lanes.
