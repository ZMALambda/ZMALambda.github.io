1.) | Political System - Parliamentary Government

  • Switch to a parliamentary democracy, wherein a Prime Minister is head of government and head of state. Furthermore, give the Supreme Advisor and Prime Minister equal power in the executive. The Supreme Advisor shall act as a power that contradicts the Prime Minister's decision based on scientific evidence.
  • The Prime Minister shall be elected by the party or majority that holds more than 50% of a unicameral parliament. Should there be no majority party or majority, the Supreme Advisor shall appoint a Prime Minister with supervision of Parliament.
  • The Supreme Advisor shall be elected by Supreme Court justices as well as the Chief Justice who all hold equal voting power as they convene as a committee that shall determine the Supreme Advisor. The Supreme Advisor has a term of 10 years with the possibility of extension to be determined by the Supreme Court. The Commission of Elections shall determine the candidates based on the qualifications listed below. As they transmit the candidates, they must redact their names in order to eliminate potential bias. This is partly inspired by the system utilized by the Vatican in order to elect a Pope, commonly known as blind voting. The qualifications to be nominated as a candidate must be:
    1. The person nominated must be able and willing to take the role of Supreme Advisor;
    2. The person holds a master's degree in economics, law, political science, or public administration;
    3. The person must not have any corruption of graft cases that resulted in conviction;
    4. The person has experience in both the executive and parliamentary branches of government; and
    5. The person has a spectacular track record, preferably a mayor who reduced corruption in his city of mayoralty.
  • Any politician who has an up to 2nd degree family member in a position is barred from running.
  • In the case that a gridlock occurs where the Supreme Advisor and Prime Minister fails to agree upon an issue, the Parliament first discusses on what type the issue is. Then, the Parliament elects a committee of 10 based on the issue to determine the ultimate outcome.
