2.) | Political System - Federal Republic

  • The Philippines shall be a federal republic because it does not have much of an established identity. In the colonial era, the Spanish, instead of dividing among ethnic lines, united the Philippine Islands under one colony. As time moved on, this motion never changed. However, as separatism grows in the Philippines in the contemporary era, it is important that we give autonomy to other ethnicities. Not only does this further unite the Philippines, but it also allows for development in other provinces. Other regions can practice their own political beliefs without harming others. They can see whether or not a policy works or not.
  • The regional governance of the Philippines shall be divided among ethnic lines. Within these ethnic lines, lower governments are established with their own constitutions similar to Germany's form of federation.
  • These lower governments shall elect officials to the Parliament to represent their population. The amount of officials these divisions can possess are determined by the population of the division. If a division has a population of 5,000,000, then it shall have 5 officials to Parliament.
  • Each division uses the language commonly spoken within their borders. As an example, if there is an Ilocano region, then it's official language shall be Ilocano. This shall hopefully prevent ethnolinguistic division within the Philippines.
  • The provinces that have already been established shall stay and join their corresponding division. The borders of the provinces can be amended in order to fit the regional borders.
  • The National Capital Region shall be expanded according to the urban expansions that have been done ever since it's conception. It shall have Filipino and English as it's main languages.
  • Global cities shall be invested upon in the Philippines and be integrated into the federal level. One example of a city being integrated into the federal level is Washington D.C.
  • Each province in the Philippines shall have a capital city and a municipality. The capital city's government shall have higher privileges than a municipal government. This already exists in our contemporary government.
