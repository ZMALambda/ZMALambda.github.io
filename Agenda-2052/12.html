12.) | Environment

  • Gas stations must be required to have a portion of their space allocated to electric car refueling stations in order to attract people to buying electric vehicles. Not only does this reduce the rate of carbon emissions, it also helps attract electric vehicle manufacturers to open markets here in the Philippines and to attract foreign investments.
  • The Philippine government should supply jeepney drivers with electric jeepneys that are manufactured here in the Philippines using state-built factories in order to assist in the transition from traditional jeepneys to jeepneys that do not emit pollution.
  • As a result of the establishment of rail networks, traffic that occurs in Metro Manila should ease and carbon emissions from idle cars would ideally lessen.
  • Abolish our extremely high dependency on coal and start gruadually leaning to better and efficient options such as nuclear energy, solar energy, wind energy, or possibly in the future, fusion energy.
  • Establish a "Department Of Mitigation" which helps reduce litter and trash. Together with the Philippine National Police, it shall hunt down not only trash in areas like the Pasig River, but also in the streets.
  • Littering should be given a more serious penalty in order to discourage littering.
  • The people should be educated on where to throw away certain trash depending on what class they belong to, whether biodegradable or non-biodegradable.
  • A military force should be deployed in the Sierra Madre in order to contain illegal mining operations. Furthermore, mining operations that have been approved in the government should be terminated immediately.
  • For every tree that is cut down, a tree must be planted by the company. To effectively watch over these, logging companies shall only be permitted to a certain area and must manage the population of trees in that area effectively throughout years. This creates an ecosystem to hopefully fix deforestation in the Philippines.
  • Flood control barriers should take an approach similar to Japan in that they use concrete that is tested and can be tested by the public in order to test it's resistance and ability to deflect water.
