5.) | Energy

  • The Philippines must choose cleaner and generally more efficient forms of energy. Therefore, we should transition from coal, oil, and other energy sources that are being utilized to other energy sources.
  • The Philippines should nationalize all energy. Instead of giving power of managing electricity to the Lopez family's MERALCO, we should instead nationalize it in order to serve it's customers rather than the owners and investors.
  • The Philippines should allow people to install solar panels on their roofs. We should have this liberty in the first place without question since other countries permit it.
  • Nuclear power should be invested upon. However, since it has been abandoned, decommissioned, outdated, and was built without sufficient research on geological activity, we should instead opt for another nuclear power plant instead of the Bataan Nuclear Power Plant.
  • If, by the time 2052 arrives, fusion reactors have become mainstream among superpowers, the Philippines should instead cooperate with it's allies and do research on it.
  • With the low costs of fusion, nuclear, solar, or wind energy, along with the nationalisation of electricity, the Philippine government should opt to make the prices as low as possible or even free.
  • Traditional wires that are clumped together without care should slowly be dismantled. These spaghetti wires does not only look terrible, it can cause harm such as a fire. Instead, the government should limit wires that are above ground to rural areas only, whilst mandating for underground wiring in urbanized areas. Even so, the government should still maintain these wires to a certain extent that it does not become a hazard for rural communities.
