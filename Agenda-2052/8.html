8.) | Civil Rights

  • The Sexual Orientation And Gender Identity or Expression Equality Act (SOGIE Act) should be passed into law to promote equality. It allows for marriage between same-sex couples and the right to receive transitional surgery.
  • Abortion should be a right given to women. However, it should only be limited to women who are in need of such an operation right away and women whose families petition for it.
  • Civil rights should be protected at all costs. It should be an impeachable offense if a sitting executive directly orders actions that break protected civil liberties.
  • A sex offender list should exist in the Philippines similar to the American sex offender system in which people found guilty with any crime relating to sexual activity can either choose to go to prison or be registered in the sex offender list which limits them off certain areas such as educational institutions that are below college and playgrounds.
  • Rape laws should be expanded upon in the Philippines in order to prevent rape crimes. Furthermore, these crimes should have more severe penalties upon conviction, such as life in prison without possibility of parole.
  • The Philippine people can choose to oust a president or dissolve the parliamentary if a 6/7 majority plurality vote is achieved.
  • The Philippine people should be encouraged to partake in protests and revolution if they deem the government not worthy enough.
  • If in any case the worst case scenario happens and protesters are shot by the authorities, the Supreme Court, under the responsibility given to it by the Constitution, should choose immediately to oust the president and dissolve Parliament. An interim/caretaker government should be formed by the Supreme Court forthwith and snap elections shall be called.
