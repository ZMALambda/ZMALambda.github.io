4.) | Political System - Federal Republic

  • Education shall be the top civil priority of the nation.
  • The education system must be reformed in order to make students learn more from a young age. Below age 10, as the brain of children grow, they can learn easier. Therefore, it is best if we feed children more education from a young age.
  • All age limits shall be removed and children should be encouraged to start their education at age 2, at a time where they have already learned to speak and somewhat communicate.
  • The "No Child Left Behind" policy shall be abolished in order to make children work harder in order to attain promotion.
  • The Department Of Education, in order to raise funding, shall give up most of it's schools in favour of a charter school system.
  • In turn, the Department Of Education shall impose strict rules on some private schools in order to minimize tuition as much as possible, even comparing to public school tuition fees.
  • The Department Of Education shall fund schools as well as promote them according to their proficiency in education. This builds a system of competition within schools, breeding innovation to hopefully reinvent how education in performed in this country.
  • Sexual education should be implemented in order to educate children on the dangers of early and unprotected sexual intercourse. It helps to prevent not only sexually transmitted diseases such as AIDS-HIV, but it also prevents underage pregnancies which is a common trigger of poverty.
  • Disciplinary education shall be mandated in all schools. This disciplinary education shall be treated as a special subject that shall merge with Values. The disciplinary education shall adopt these agendas:
    1. Citizenry - This shall be focused on building well-mannered and lawful citizens. It focuses on matters such as teamwork, heroicism, upon other matters.
    2. Lawfulness - This shall educate children on how to behave well and to avoid crime. Fear tactics to a certain degree shall be adopted in order to instill fear in the future generation and to hopefully reduce crime.
    3. Prevention - This shall educate children to prevent addiction. These addictions include alcohol, drugs, pornography, vulgarity, upon other things. This is done in order to prevent children from growing disarray from their lives.
  • Another subject that should be integrated with the education system is the knowledge of technology, specifically Artificial Intelligence and Robotics. This builds a generation that is technologically literate.
  • The K-12 education system shall be amended by champions of education as the current system as it is has failed to serve it's very purpose, to educate children further and to prepare them for jobs even without degrees. This is evident as ever since it's implementation, more than 19 million senior high school graduates are illiterate, defined as being hindered to read, speak, or write.
  • The scholarship programs shall be expanded to include everyone except high-earners. This is to hopefully get special students who are from the middle class yet cannot enter scholarships since they can barely pay off their tuitions.
  • A mandatory Intelligence Quotient test must be present in all educational institutions in order to differ those who require more attention that those who do not.
  • Sections should be separated with differing Intelligence Quotient values. The method of teaching should also be dynamic with the learning capabilities of the pupils in such a section.
