6.) | Transportation

  • The Philippine government, instead of limiting their focus to road networks, should also focus on train networks.
  • Metropolitan Manila is one of the most populated metropolis in the entire world. However, unlike cities like Tokyo which have many metro networks that connect it's vast lands and population, Metropolitan Manila is limited to only 3 train networks that span it's lands. There are plans for 2 more train networks, one that goes to San Jose Del Monte in Bulacan and other that spans from South to North. However, for it's population, 5 is still very lacking. Therefore, the Philippines should mandate for more trains and less cars.
  • As the Philippines invests more into metro networks, it should also, to a certain degree, invest in other forms of public transportation. Examples include trams, buses, and favorably, monorails. With all of these forms of public transportation combined, not only does the amount of traffic lessen, the common person can have less of a burden on their financial lives. In other words, they will no longer need to worry for the purchase and management of a motor vehicle.
  • A lengthy highway should be built that spans from Ilocos all the way to Batangas where it shall split into two parts, one going to Palawan and the other going to the Eastern coast going down until Davao. However, if technology that allows for this to be built is not present, it should not be continued.
  • On the other hand, a rail network should be established along the path of this highway. It should preferably be a tram in between the lanes in order to reduce costs significantly as opposed to a traditional train.
  • The Philippines is infamous not only for it's beautiful nature, but also for it's potholes. The Department of Public Works and Highways should dedicate some of their workforce to combating these potholes.
  • If in the near future, technology is more advanced and cheaper, speed bumps should be replaced in favor of smart systems that automatically detects when a person is above the speed limit and deploys methods to slow them down. Alternatively, instead of speed bumps, Newtonian fluids can be stored inside speed bumps to become liquid when a car is below the speed limit this allowing for a smooth crossover and to become solid when a car is over the speed limit to slow them down.
  • Highways with no speed limits should be implemented in the Philippines similar to the Autobahn.
  • Toll booths should be abolished in order to make traveling across the country generally more accessible to the common people.
